## Final prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a finished 1536x1024 landscape Steam Workshop cover for a RimWorld addon. Reference 1 character identities only: orange-haired large fox-eared character with braids and fluffy pale tails, and small pale olive-blond-haired character wearing a red scarf and dark coat. Reference 2 character identity only: long black hair, round ears and blue horn-like ear accents, glasses, tan robe with cream sash. Reference 3 graphic style only: dark chocolate background, warm beige bold RimTalk heading, retro pixel illustration and pale pixel speech bubble. Show all three recognizable chibi RimWorld-style characters gathered around a glowing native stone-ring campfire at night sharing stories, friendly cozy mood, blocky pixel-art treatment and crisp thick outlines. Do not reproduce screenshot interface or bed. Strong readable upper title exactly 'Rimtalk' and subtitle exactly '篝火的故事'. Small secondary English subtitle 'Campfire Stories'. A small speech bubble contains a simple heart icon, no additional text. Rich warm amber firelight with restrained cool blue accents, dark brown background. Composition clear at thumbnail size, professional unified cover, not a collage, no watermark.

## Inspection

The selected image shows all three character references, a central stone-ring fire, the requested title and the English subtitle. No game HUD or screenshot background remains. The character rendering is an interpretation of the references rather than an exact sprite reproduction.
